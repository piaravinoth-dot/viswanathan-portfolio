Case Study / AI Design Studio

Editable AI design, not flat AI output.

An internal AI-assisted creative workspace built from a designer’s production workflow — ideation, editable composition, layer control, typography, revision and final output.

RoleDesign + Product thinking
FocusEditable workflow
ToolsFigma + AI + code
OutputProduction workspace
Problem

AI tools create images. Designers need control.

Most AI creative tools generate a final flat image. That helps with speed, but it does not solve real design production needs: changing text, moving elements, controlling hierarchy, replacing images, correcting layouts and exporting final creatives for review.

01Flat outputs

Generated images are difficult to revise when feedback comes in.

02Design control

Designers need layers, text control, image positioning and predictable layout behaviour.

03Production speed

The workflow must help teams move faster without losing craft and quality.

Process

Built around actual creative production.

The product direction was shaped from practical design work: receiving content, planning the first layout, generating alternatives, reviewing quality, applying corrections and preparing final output.

STEP 01

Workflow mapping

Mapped the designer journey from brief to review-ready creative.

STEP 02

Editable canvas thinking

Defined a canvas model where text, image and layout elements remain adjustable.

STEP 03

AI assistance layer

Used AI for ideation, variation and production support while keeping designer control central.

STEP 04

Output and review flow

Focused on reusable production logic, preview and export readiness.

Outcome

Designer-first AI workflow.

Speed with control.

The strongest value is not simply generating more visuals. It is bringing AI assistance into a familiar editable workflow so designers can create, refine and deliver faster without losing control over craft, hierarchy and production quality.
