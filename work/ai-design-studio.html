The Challenge

AI can generate. Production needs control.

Most AI image tools end at a flat output. Real design work continues after generation: text changes, asset replacement, hierarchy corrections, cropping, transforms, layout refinement, review cycles and final export. The opportunity was to make AI part of that workflow instead of treating generation as the finished product.

01Flat output problem

Generated creatives become difficult to revise once feedback starts.

02Designer control

Text, images, layers, position, crop and transforms need to remain editable.

03Production reality

The system had to support speed without sacrificing visual hierarchy or quality control.

My Role

Designed from the workflow outward.

I approached the product from a designer’s perspective: what happens after a brief arrives, where time is lost, what changes repeatedly, and what must remain under human control. The product direction, interface logic and refinement model were shaped around those recurring production decisions.

Workflow

Brief to editable creative.

The system was developed around the full creative journey rather than a single generation moment.

STEP 01

Receive content and references

Start from a real brief, content requirement and visual direction rather than an isolated prompt.

STEP 02

Generate a first composition

Use AI to accelerate ideation and initial layout direction.

STEP 03

Edit on canvas

Keep text, images, layers, placement, crop and transforms adjustable.

STEP 04

Refine and review

Apply corrections, visual QA and local changes without rebuilding the creative.

STEP 05

Prepare final output

Move from editable state to production-ready preview and export.

System Thinking

AI assists. The designer stays in control.

The core product idea is a controlled relationship between generation and editing. AI helps accelerate creation, while the workspace preserves the structure needed for professional design work.

CANVASEditable composition

Elements remain individually adjustable instead of being baked into one final image.

LAYERSStructured control

Design elements can be selected, reordered and refined with predictable behaviour.

REFINEMENTLocal changes

Corrections can be applied without restarting the entire creative workflow.

OUTPUTProduction-ready flow

The workspace connects ideation, editing, review and export in one system.
