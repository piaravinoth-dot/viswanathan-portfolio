01 / THE CHALLENGE

Generation
is just a start.

AI can create a first visual quickly. Real production needs editable text, images, layers, positioning, cropping and revision control.

Designer control is not an optional feature. It is the foundation of a useful AI-assisted creative product.

Instead of ending at a flattened image, the workflow supports the corrections and feedback that happen after the first composition. Designers retain control over the creative, rather than starting over every time something changes.
