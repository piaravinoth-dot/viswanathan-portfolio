AI Design Studio

An editable AI-assisted creative workspace built around real production workflows — designed to keep the speed of AI without giving up designer control.

The problem

Conventional AI design tools often stop at a flat generated image. Production teams still need to edit copy, swap imagery, adjust layout, manage layers, review revisions and export final assets. The gap between generation and production becomes the workflow bottleneck.

The approach

Editable output

Text, imagery, layers and positioning stay adjustable after generation.

Workflow-first UX

The interface follows how real creative teams receive, refine, review and release work.

AI as assistant

Generation, variation and refinement support the designer instead of replacing control.

My role combined product concept, UX/UI, design-system thinking and AI workflow design. The product evolved stage by stage from local design planning and editable canvas composition to persistence, authentication, remote storage and production-provider integration.

Core product system

Canvas editing, layers, text and image controls, variation generation, local refinement, previews, PNG export, project persistence and authenticated access were treated as one connected production experience. The technical workflow also explored MCP connectors, LangChain and Codex-assisted development.
