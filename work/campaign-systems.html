01 / THE CHALLENGE

Consistency
without sameness.

High-volume communication needs enough structure for speed and recognition, with flexibility for each message and format.

A campaign is not one poster. It is a system that must survive different messages, audiences and deadlines.

Typography, hierarchy, image treatment and reusable rules create continuity while allowing intentional composition across digital, editorial, presentation and physical formats.

02 / THE METHOD

One idea.
