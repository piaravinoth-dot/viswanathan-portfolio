CASE STUDY / 02

Political Consulting Website

A responsive website system that translated a complex consulting business into a clear, credible and reusable digital experience — from structure and wireframes through Figma and Codex-assisted implementation.

The challenge

The website needed to explain a political consulting and analytics offering without overwhelming users. The work required clear information architecture, a strong professional visual language, reusable page patterns and responsive behaviour across desktop and mobile.

From structure to UI

Information architecture

Organized services, capabilities, proof points and calls-to-action into a clear page hierarchy.

Responsive design

Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts were designed as one fluid system.

Reusable components

Navigation, content blocks, media sections and cards were built for consistent reuse.

The project expanded my role beyond campaign communication into digital product and website design: user flows, page hierarchy, wireframes, responsive UI, Figma prototyping and developer-ready structure.

Design to implementation

The approved Figma direction was translated into a production-ready responsive website through a Codex-assisted build workflow. Design intent, reusable components and visual consistency were preserved through the handoff rather than recreated independently in code.
